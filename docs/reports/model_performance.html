How to read this

  • Trained on 48 districts and scored on the 48 in the study area, out of 3103 in the dataset.
  • Method: 5-fold cross-validation grouped by local authority. Errors are in visits per 1,000 residents, on out-of-fold predictions.

  • The model is scored on only 48 districts, so the error figures are themselves uncertain.
  • The final model and the ensemble members are chosen on the same folds they are scored on, so the winning score is slightly optimistic.
